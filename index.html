lock Faq

Cybersecurity questions answered clearly

What is a Zero-Trust Architecture?

keyboard_arrow_down

Zero-Trust is a security framework requiring all users, whether in or outside the organization's network, to be authenticated, authorized, and continuously validated before being granted or keeping access to applications and data. It operates on the principle of "never trust, always verify."

How does a SOC (Security Operations Center) work?

keyboard_arrow_down

Our SOC functions as a centralized command hub where our cybersecurity experts monitor, assess, and defend your enterprise information systems 24/7. We utilize advanced AI and threat intelligence to detect anomalies and neutralize threats before they can cause a breach.

Why is Penetration Testing necessary?

keyboard_arrow_down

Penetration testing simulates a real-world cyberattack against your systems to identify exploitable vulnerabilities. By finding these weak points before malicious actors do, we can patch them and harden your infrastructure against actual threats.

What happens during a ransomware attack?

keyboard_arrow_down

During a ransomware attack, malicious software encrypts your critical data, and attackers demand a ransom for the decryption key. Our mitigation strategy focuses on aggressive endpoint protection to block the malware, combined with isolated, immutable backups to ensure data can be restored without paying the ransom.

How do you secure cloud infrastructure?

keyboard_arrow_down

We secure cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP) by implementing strict Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls, data encryption at rest and in transit, micro-segmentation of networks, and continuous compliance monitoring to prevent unauthorized data exfiltration.
